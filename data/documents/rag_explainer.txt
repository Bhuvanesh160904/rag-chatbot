Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI technique that combines information retrieval with text generation. Instead of relying only on what a language model learned during training, RAG retrieves relevant documents or text chunks from an external knowledge base at query time, then uses that retrieved context to generate a more accurate, grounded answer. This helps reduce hallucination and allows the model to answer questions about information it was never trained on, such as private company documents or recent events.
